Nearby Talk

Talk without the internet.

A voice room between iPhones that are physically close. No account, no signal needed: your phone finds the phones around it over Wi‑Fi, Wi‑Fi Aware or Bluetooth and talks to them directly, with no server. Friends who are far away can join over the internet, end‑to‑end encrypted. Made for the concert, the plane, the Tube, the slope.
